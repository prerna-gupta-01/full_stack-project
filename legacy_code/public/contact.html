Frequently Asked Questions

Click on "Report Lost Item" from the navigation bar or dashboard. Fill in the item details including name, description, location where it was last seen, and optionally upload a photo. Your report will be visible to everyone on the platform.

Browse the "Found Items" page and find your item. Click on it to view details, then click the "Claim This Item" button. Provide a description of how you can prove the item belongs to you. The finder will review your claim.

You will receive a notification. Go to the item details page to view the claim. You can approve or reject the claim. If approved, the item status will change to "Claimed" and you can coordinate the return via the provided contact information.

You can bring found items to the Lost & Found office located in the Campus Admin Building, Room 101. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. You can also report the item online first and then drop it off.

Found items are kept in the Lost & Found office for 90 days. After that, unclaimed items may be donated or disposed of. We recommend reporting and checking regularly.
